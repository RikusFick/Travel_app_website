A 11-stop offline audio guide to Al Fahidi and Dubai Creek in Dubai, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers coral-stone wind-tower lanes, heritage courtyards, and an abra crossing on Dubai Creek. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.
