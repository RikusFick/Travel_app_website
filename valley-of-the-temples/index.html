    Read along

    You're now looking at the Valley of the Temples. It isn't a valley, though. This is a ridge, a natural rampart, with the ancient city spread behind it and the sea in front.

    The city was called Akragas. Greek settlers from Gela, on Sicily's south coast, founded it around 580 BC, joined by colonists from Crete and Rhodes. It was the last of the great Greek colonies in Sicily, and it became one of the richest.

    The temple in front of you is traditionally called the Temple of Juno, or Hera. In fact, apart from one temple, all the names here are guesses made in the Renaissance. This one dates to around 450 BC, set on a huge artificial terrace.

    Ancient writers claimed Akragas had two hundred thousand inhabitants. Modern historians think thirty to forty thousand is closer.
