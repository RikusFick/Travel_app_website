Privacy policy

Эта страница на английском. Действует английская версия.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how FINICK R EN J (Pty) Ltd (registered in South Africa with CIPC, registration number 2025/324909/07, trading as Earwise; "we", "us") handles personal information when you use getearwise.com and the Earwise mobile app. We collect only what is needed to run the guides, accounts, and purchases.

1. Who is responsible

FINICK R EN J (Pty) Ltd (reg. 2025/324909/07), Kalkoven LS299, Mara, Vivo, Limpopo, South Africa, is the "responsible party" under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and the "controller" under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For any privacy question or deletion request, email hello@getearwise.com.

Information officer: Rikus Fick, Director. Postal and physical address: Kalkoven LS299, Mara, Vivo, Limpopo, South Africa. Email: hello@getearwise.com.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no third-party ad networks, and no advertising IDs.
  • No website analytics trackers or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not track or store your walking route or location history on any server.

4. Hosting and service providers

We rely on a small set of infrastructure providers to run Earwise: GitHub Pages (website hosting, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security), Cloudflare R2 (audio and media delivery), OpenStreetMap and jsDelivr (website map tiles and Leaflet library), Google Fonts (typography), Web3Forms (website contact form delivery), Firebase Authentication (Google, for account sign-in), Cloudflare Workers and KV (purchase records), Paystack (website payments), and Apple and Google (app distribution and in-app purchases, coming soon). Some providers process data outside South Africa or the EEA; where they do, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses and POPIA section 72.

5. How long we keep data

We keep support emails and contact-form messages for up to 24 months after the issue is closed and then delete them. Account records remain active while you have an account, until you ask us to delete them. Purchase and invoice records are kept for as long as South African tax and accounting laws require.

6. Your rights (POPIA & GDPR)

Under POPIA and the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy in a clear, portable format.
  • Correct inaccurate or out-of-date details.
  • Delete your Earwise account and support emails (except records we are legally required to keep for tax or fraud prevention).
  • Object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent (for example, turning off location access in your phone's settings at any time).

How to delete your account or data: you can delete your Earwise sign-in from your account page. That removes your login. Purchase and invoice records are kept as required for accounting. You can also email hello@getearwise.com from the email address linked to your account, or send a message through the contact form on our support page, and tell us what you want deleted. We will confirm once it is done.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) (inforegulator.org.za) or with your local EU/UK data protection authority.

7. Children

Earwise provides general-interest travel guides and is not aimed at children under 16 (or under 18 without a parent or guardian's consent under POPIA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Connections to getearwise.com, Cloudflare R2, Firebase, and Paystack use HTTPS encryption. Payment card processing is delegated to Apple, Google, or Paystack so card details are never stored on our servers.

9. Changes

When we update this policy, for instance when the mobile apps launch, we will change the date at the top of this page.
