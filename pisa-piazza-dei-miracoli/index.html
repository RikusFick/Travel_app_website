    Read along

    You're looking at Pisa's cathedral square.

    In front of you, laid out on the grass, are four great white buildings: the round Baptistery, the cathedral, the bell tower, which is the Leaning Tower, and, along the northern wall, the monumental cemetery, the Camposanto.

    Officially this is just the Cathedral Square. But in 1910, the poet Gabriele D'Annunzio called it the "meadow of miracles" in one of his novels, and the name stuck: the Piazza dei Miracoli.

    In 1987, the whole square was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
