A 11-stop offline audio guide to Miletus in Balat, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers the sweeping Roman theatre, Faustina Baths, and port ruins of Ionian Miletus. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.

Stops

  1. 1

    The theatre

    At a permitted viewing point by the theatre; do not enter closed seating or passages. This is stop 1 on the route.

  2. 2

    The fortress above the theatre

    From a permitted path with a view of the fortress above the theatre; climbing is not required. This is stop 2 on the route.

  3. 3

    The Lion Harbour

    At a permitted viewing edge near the Harbour Monument; use manual play if low ground is inaccessible. This is stop 3 on the route.

    The Delphinion

    From a permitted path overlooking the Delphinion; the pin identifies the sanctuary area, not its entrance. This is stop 4 on the route.

  5. 5

    The Ionic Stoa and the processional avenue

    From a permitted path beside the Ionic Stoa; the pin identifies the avenue area, not a guaranteed dry route. This is stop 5 on the route.

  6. 6

    The Nymphaeum

    From a permitted viewing edge near the Nymphaeum; no entry to basins is required. This is stop 6 on the route.

  7. 7

    The Market Gate

    From a permitted viewing edge near the low foundation of the Market Gate. This is stop 7 on the route.

  8. 8

    The bouleuterion

    From a permitted viewing edge at the bouleuterion; entering the seats is not required. This is stop 8 on the route.

  9. 9

    The Temple of Serapis

    From a permitted viewing edge at the Temple of Serapis. This is stop 9 on the route.

  10. 10

    The Baths of Faustina

  11. 11

    The İlyas Bey Mosque

    From a permitted point in front of the İlyas Bey Mosque; interior access is not assumed. This is stop 11 on the route.
