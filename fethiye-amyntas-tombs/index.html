A 3-stop offline audio guide to Amyntas Tombs in Fethiye, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers the cliffside Lycian rock-cut tombs of ancient Telmessos overlooking Fethiye. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.

Stops

  1. 1

    A city of the dead above the town

    At the foot of the cliff, by the path up to the rock tombs, with the temple-like façades above you. This is stop 1 on the route.

  2. 2

    The King's Tomb

    On the terrace in front of the largest tomb, with its two columns and triangular roof. This is stop 2 on the route.

  3. 3

    The smaller tombs and the view

    On the path beside the smaller rock tombs, looking out over Fethiye and its bay. This is stop 3 on the route.
