    Read along

    The Cour des Adieux, the Courtyard of Farewells, opens out in front of you, with wings from several centuries around it.

    For about eight hundred years kings and emperors lived at Fontainebleau, and each one changed it to his taste. On the left, the wing marked with the letter F of François I dates from the Renaissance; on the right is a wing of the eighteenth century. Napoleon later called the palace the true home of kings, the house of centuries.

    Its older name is the Courtyard of the White Horse. The open side, closed by iron railings, was once a fourth wing, the Ferrara wing.
