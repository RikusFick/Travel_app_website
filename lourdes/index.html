    The Grotto of Massabielle is a shallow cave in the rock above the river Gave, and the reason Lourdes became one of the best-known pilgrimage places in the world.

    On 11 February 1858, a fourteen-year-old girl from a poor local family, Bernadette Soubirous, came here to gather wood. She later said that she saw a young lady dressed in white in the niche of the rock. By her account, the visions came eighteen times between February and July that year.

    The statue in the niche marks the spot where she said the lady appeared.

    The rock is called Massabielle, from the local words for old rock, and it stands in a loop of the Gave de Pau, the fast river that comes down from Gavarnie in the high Pyrenees. Bernadette was the eldest child of François Soubirous, a miller, and his wife Louise. By 1858 the family had fallen so far that they lived in a single room in a disused jail, known as the cachot, the dungeon. The statue in the niche was carved by the Lyon sculptor Joseph-Hugues Fabisch and dedicated on the fourth of April 1864 before a crowd of about twenty thousand. Bernadette is said to have found it too big and too old to be the lady she had seen.

    At first Bernadette called the figure simply Aquero, a local word meaning that one. On the twenty-fifth of March 1858, after she had asked her name several times, the lady answered in the local Occitan tongue: Que soy era Immaculada Councepciou, I am the Immaculate Conception. The words are inscribed in an arc at the foot of the statue. Pius IX had proclaimed the Immaculate Conception a dogma of the Church only four years earlier, in 1854. It is a tradition to come to the grotto with a blessed candle, in memory of the candle Bernadette held during the visions, and the smoke of countless candles has blackened the rock.

    She entered the convent of Saint-Gildard in Nevers in July 1866, at twenty-two, and took the name Sister Marie-Bernard. Writers soon followed the pilgrims, among them Émile Zola, whose novel Lourdes deals with faith and healing, and Joris-Karl Huysmans.
