A 11-stop offline audio guide to Perge in Aksu, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers the massive Roman stadium, Hellenistic gate towers, colonnaded street, and baths. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.

Stops

  1. 1

    The stadium

    Beside the long, U-shaped stadium, on the approach road before the city gates. This is stop 1 on the route.

  2. 2

    The theatre

    On the approach road, beside the theatre built into the hillside on the west side of the road, a short distance from the stadium. This is stop 2 on the route.

  3. 3

    A remarkable woman

    Outside the Roman gate in the city walls, near the tomb of Plancia Magna. This is stop 3 on the route.

  4. 4

    The Propylon

    Between the Roman gate and the round towers, in front of the east facade of the Southern Baths, beside the monumental gateway (propylon). This is stop 4 on the route.

  5. 5

    The Southern Baths

    In the large bath complex just west of the Hellenistic gate, among high walls of brick and stone, with the stubby brick pillars of the heating system visible below floor level. This is stop 5 on the route.

  6. 6

    The Hellenistic gate

    Stand here for the story of The Hellenistic gate.

  7. 7

    The agora

    Beside the square market building (agora), just east of the Hellenistic gate. This is stop 7 on the route.

  8. 8

    The colonnaded street

    On the main colonnaded street, beside the stone water channel that runs down its centre. This is stop 8 on the route.

  9. 9

    The crossroads

    At the crossroads near the top of the colonnaded street, where the western street branches off to the left. This is stop 9 on the route.

  10. 10

    The palaestra of Cornutus

    Beside the large square building on the north side of the western street. This is stop 10 on the route.

  11. 11

    The fountain at the head of the street

    At the top of the colonnaded street, in front of the monumental fountain (nymphaeum) at the foot of the acropolis hill. This is stop 11 on the route.
