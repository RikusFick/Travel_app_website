A 5-stop offline audio guide to Marrakech souks and northern medina in Marrakech, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers the spice stalls of Rahba Kedima, dyers’ lanes, and the Almoravid Qubba dome. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.

Stops

  1. 1

    The mouth of Souk Semmarine

    At the north side of Jemaa el-Fna, under the arch where Souk Semmarine begins. This is stop 1 on the route.

  2. 2

    The Mouassine Mosque and fountain

    In front of the Mouassine Fountain, beside the mosque. This is stop 2 on the route.

  3. 3

    Le Jardin Secret

    Outside the entrance of Le Jardin Secret on Rue Mouassine. This is stop 3 on the route.

  4. 4

    Chrob ou Chouf, the Saadian wall fountain

    In front of the Chrob ou Chouf wall fountain. This is stop 4 on the route.

  5. 5

    The Almoravid Qubba

    At the railing above the sunken Almoravid Qubba. This is stop 5 on the route.
