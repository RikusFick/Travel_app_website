Nine offline stories pair with ferry crossings or lakeside drives across central Lake Como, from Como's waterfront up to Bellagio and Varenna. Hear stories of Pliny the Younger, Alpine glaciers, silk villas, and hillside gardens.

Stops

  1. 1

    Volta's lake

    Start on Como's promenade beside the neoclassical Volta Temple, honouring physicist Alessandro Volta and the glacial Y-shaped lake.

  2. 2

    Pliny's comedy and tragedy

    Look back at Como's waterfront where Roman writer Pliny the Younger grew up nearly two thousand years ago.

  3. 3

    The cursed island

    Pass wooded Isola Comacina, destroyed by rival Como in 1169 and long steeped in local legend.

  4. 4

    The explorer's villa

    Admire Villa del Balbianello at the tip of the Lavedo peninsula, known for its lakeside loggia and mountaineer Guido Monzino's collection.

  5. 5

    Villa Carlotta

    Stop at Tremezzo's Villa Carlotta, famous for spring azaleas, terraced gardens, and neoclassical sculptures inside.

  6. 6

    Bellagio

    Arrive at Bellagio on the rocky promontory dividing Lake Como's two southern branches.

  7. 7

    Villa Melzi

    Stroll Villa Melzi's lakeside gardens south of Bellagio among cypresses, Japanese maples, and Dante sculptures.

  8. 8

    Varenna and the convent villa

    Cross to Varenna's colourful waterfront and Villa Monastero, a former twelfth-century convent turned botanical garden.

  9. 9

    Castello di Vezio

    Climb above Varenna to the medieval watchtower of Castello di Vezio for sweeping views over the central lake.
