Explore Andalusia's dramatic cliff-top town cleaved in two by the 120-metre chasm of El Tajo. Seven stops take you across the soaring arches of Puente Nuevo, past Spain's historic stone bullring, along cliffside gardens, and down to the medieval Arab Baths.
