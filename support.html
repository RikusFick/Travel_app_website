Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

If you bought inside the app, tap Restore purchases in Settings. If you bought on the website, sign in to the app with the same email you used at checkout. If it still does not show, send us a message with your account email.

Can I listen in my browser as well as in the app?

Yes. Guides you buy on the website play here in your browser, with the read-along script. Sign in to the app with the same email and they unlock there too, and in the app the story starts as you arrive.

What currency will I be charged in?

Guide prices on the website are shown in euros and billed in South African rand at the day’s exchange rate; your bank converts. In-app purchases are charged by Apple or Google in the store’s currency. See our terms of use.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are explained in our terms of use. In-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google Play. For a purchase made on getearwise.com, send us a message or see the terms for how to request a review.

How do I delete my account or personal data?

See our privacy policy for your rights and how to ask us to delete your account or data, or send a message from your account email.
