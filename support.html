Frequently asked questions

Can I listen offline on the website, or only in the app?

Offline guides are app-only. Guides bought on the website play online in your browser (with the read-along script) whenever you have a connection. Offline listening and automatic GPS playback at each stop need the free Earwise app signed in with the same account.

Are the iPhone and Android apps or website checkout open yet?

Not yet. The apps are not in the App Store or Google Play yet, and "Buy — coming soon" buttons on guide pages email hello@getearwise.com instead of taking payment. You can already listen to free guides and stop-1 samples of paid guides on the website.

How do guides I buy on the website unlock in the app?

When checkout and sign-in go live, purchases on getearwise.com are tied to your Earwise account. Sign in to the free Earwise app with the same email address to download those guides for offline use and GPS auto-play.

Stories don’t start by themselves in the app

Check that location permission is set to "While using the app", that auto-play is enabled in Settings, and that the guide has been downloaded.

A purchase doesn’t show up in the app

If you bought inside the app, tap "Restore purchases" in Settings. If you bought on the website, check that you are signed in with the same email address you used at checkout, or email hello@getearwise.com.

How do refunds work?

In-app purchases are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (Order history). For purchases made on getearwise.com, email hello@getearwise.com within 14 days with your account email and guide name.

How do I delete my account or personal data?

Email hello@getearwise.com from your account email address. See our privacy policy for full details.

How do I report a wrong fact or misplaced map pin?

Email hello@getearwise.com with the guide name and stop number, and we will verify and update it.
