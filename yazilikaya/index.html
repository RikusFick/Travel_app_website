A 3-stop offline audio guide to Yazılıkaya in Boğazkale, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers the open-air Hittite rock sanctuary carved with processions of storm gods and kings. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.

Stops

  1. 1

    The sanctuary gateway

    At a safe, permitted outdoor viewing position for the sanctuary entrance area; do not stand on ancient foundations. This is stop 1 on the route.

  2. 2

    Chamber A: the procession of the gods

    At a safe, permitted viewing position for Chamber A, facing toward the meeting scene; entry is not assumed. This is stop 2 on the route.

  3. 3

    Chamber B: the king and the underworld

    At a safe, permitted viewing position for Chamber B; this area reference does not guarantee entry or a particular left/right orientation. This is stop 3 on the route.
