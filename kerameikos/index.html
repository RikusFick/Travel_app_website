A 6-stop offline audio guide to Kerameikos in Athens, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers the Sacred Gate, Dipylon Gate, and funerary stelae lining the ancient Street of Tombs. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.

Stops

  1. 1

    The potters' quarter

    In the middle of the site, beside the stream bed of the Eridanos, looking over the ruins. This is stop 1 on the route.

  2. 2

    The city wall

    Beside the remains of the ancient city wall. This is stop 2 on the route.

  3. 3

    The Dipylon Gate

    At the remains of the great double gate. This is stop 3 on the route.

  4. 4

    The Pompeion

    Between the two gates, at the foundations of a large courtyard building. This is stop 4 on the route.

  5. 5

    The Street of the Tombs

    On the Street of the Tombs, among the grave monuments. This is stop 5 on the route.

    The Kerameikos Museum

    Inside the small museum at the edge of the site. (Indoors: manual play.). This is stop 6 on the route.
