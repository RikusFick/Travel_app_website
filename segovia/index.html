Walk Segovia's limestone spine from the dry-stacked granite arches of Rome to the fairy-tale prow of the Alcázar above two rivers. Seven stops highlight medieval Noble houses, Spain's last Gothic cathedral, and the castle where Isabella was proclaimed queen.

Stops

  1. 1

    The Aqueduct

    Stand beneath 167 granite arches held together without mortar since the first or second century AD.

  2. 2

    The Casa de los Picos

    Spot the 617 diamond-cut granite pyramids covering the Renaissance façade of Casa de los Picos.

  3. 3

    San Martín and Juan Bravo

    Pause on the terraced square beside Romanesque San Martín church and a sixteenth-century rebel statue.

  4. 4

    Segovia Cathedral

    Enter Segovia's late-Gothic cathedral, nicknamed the Lady of Cathedrals for its warm golden stone.

  5. 5

    The Alcázar

    Cross the deep moat to the cliff-perched fortress rebuilt under the Trastámara and Habsburg kings.

  6. 6

    The Tower of John II

    Climb past the tall keep added by John II above the confluence of the Eresma and Clamores rivers.

  7. 7

    The Hall of Kings

    Finish beneath the gilded frieze of monarchs seated around the ceiling of the Hall of Kings.
