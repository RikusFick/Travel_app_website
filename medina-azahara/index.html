Discover the short-lived tenth-century palace-city built by Caliph Abd ar-Rahman III in the foothills west of Córdoba, abandoned and looted less than eighty years after its founding. Seven stops trace the museum finds, terraced halls, and the restored reception pavilion.

Stops

  1. 1

    The Medina Azahara Museum

    Begin at the sunken museum down on the plain, where excavated capitals, pottery, and bronze stags are displayed.

  2. 2

    The Court of the Pillars and the Caliph's House

    Walk onto the upper terraces through the residential courtyard and administrative quarters.

  3. 3

    The House of Ja'far

    Inspect the white-and-pink marble arcades of the chief minister Ja'far's private courtyard house.

  4. 4

    The Great Portico and the Upper Basilical Hall

    Approach the monumental fifteen-arch Great Portico that once fronted the palace parade square.

  5. 5

    The Hall of Abd ar-Rahman III

    Learn about the richly carved Salon Rico, Abd ar-Rahman III's glittering reception hall for foreign envoys.

  6. 6

    The Upper Garden and the Central Pavilion

    Look across the sunken four-part garden terraces and central pool pavilion.

  7. 7

    The Mosque

    End beside the limestone foundations of the Aljama Mosque facing toward Córdoba.
