  1. 1

    The Ghuriya: a sultan's double complex

    On Al-Muizz Street, between the two halves of the Ghuriya, with the mosque and its minaret on the west side and the mausoleum and fountain building on the east. This is stop 1 on the route.

    Play0:00 / –:––
    Read along

    You are standing inside a sultan's monument. It has two halves, one on each side of the street.

    Its founder was Qansuh al-Ghuri. He took the throne in 1501 and ruled for fifteen years. He was one of the last Mamluk sultans of Egypt.

    His complex went up in 1503 and 1504. On the west side stand the mosque and madrasa, a school for Islamic law and the sayings of the Prophet. On the east side are his mausoleum, a lodge for Sufis, a public fountain and a Quran school above it.

    Look up. A wooden roof spans the street between the two halves. Under it runs a market for cloth and clothes.

    Now face the mosque. Its doorway is raised above the street, and shops sit below it. A mosque built this way is called a suspended mosque.

    The doorway is crowned by a three-lobed arch. Seven tiers of muqarnas, carved stone in stepped layers, fill it. The doorway also carries the sultan's blazon, set among bands of black and white marble.

    At the corner rises a square minaret of three storeys. It ends in five bulb-shaped tops, a distinctive and rare design.

    Al-Ghuri was never buried in his mausoleum. In 1516 he died at the Battle of Marj Dabiq, in northern Syria, fighting the Ottomans.

    A year later the Ottoman sultan Selim the First took Cairo. According to the chronicler Ibn Iyas, Selim admired this building greatly. He called its hall wondrous, and wished for a building like it in Istanbul.

    Selim had a darker plan too. He wanted Egypt's best craftsmen for Istanbul. His ministers met in this mosque to choose the master builders and artisans who would be sent away.

    There is one more sad link. The last Mamluk sultan, Tumanbay, was buried in a courtyard of this complex after his execution. His remains were later moved.
