A 8-stop offline audio guide to Meteora in Kalabaka, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers the sandstone rock pillars and cliff-top monasteries of Great Meteoron and Varlaam. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.

Stops

  1. 1

    The heavenly columns

    At a viewpoint on the foot road at the base of the cliffs above Kalambaka. This is stop 1 on the route.

  2. 2

    The rock of Badovas

    Near Kastraki, at the foot of the rock of Badovas and its cave monastery of St Nicholas. This is stop 2 on the route.

  3. 3

    St Nicholas Anapafsas

    At St Nicholas Anapafsas, on its rock above the monastery road. This is stop 3 on the route.

  4. 4

    The Great Meteoron

    A short story at this point covers The Great Meteoron.

  5. 5

    Varlaam

    At the viewpoint or gate below Varlaam Monastery, on the rock east of the Great Meteoron. This is stop 5 on the route.

  6. 6

    Roussanou

    At the bridges that lead up to Roussanou Monastery. This is stop 6 on the route.

  7. 7

    The Holy Trinity Monastery

    At the Holy Trinity Monastery, where a path and rock-cut steps lead up from the road. This is stop 7 on the route.

  8. 8

    St Stephen

    At the entrance to St Stephen's Monastery. This is stop 8 on the route.
