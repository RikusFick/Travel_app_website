Seven offline stories link the five cliffside fishing villages of the Ligurian coast—perfect whether you travel by train, boat, or footpath. Discover dry-stone vineyard terraces, medieval coastal raids, and each village's distinct character.

Stops

  1. 1

    Five lands

    Begin in steep Riomaggiore, where pastel tower-houses pack tightly into a narrow ravine above a tiny stone boat landing.

  2. 2

    The lovers' path

    Pause at the start of the cliff-hugging Via dell'Amore footpath linking Riomaggiore to Manarola.

  3. 3

    Manarola

    Walk down through Manarola past its fourteenth-century church and steep vineyard terraces rising above the harbour rocks.

  4. 4

    The village on the cliff

    Climb to Corniglia's sea terrace, the only Cinque Terre village perched atop a hundred-metre cliff rather than at water level.

  5. 5

    Vernazza's harbour

    Arrive in Vernazza's harbour piazza beside the sea-washed church of Santa Margherita and medieval watchtower.

  6. 6

    Monterosso

    Continue to Monterosso al Mare, largest of the five villages with a long crescent beach and old medieval centre.

  7. 7

    The Giant

    End at the far side of Fegina beach beneath the weathered fourteen-metre concrete statue of Neptune known as Il Gigante.
