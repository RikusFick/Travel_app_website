Climb the cobbled alleys of Granada's oldest Moorish quarter across the Darro valley from the Alhambra. Five stops trace medieval Arab baths, hidden cisterns, postcard viewpoints, and the hillside cave homes of Sacromonte.

Stops

  1. 1

    Carrera del Darro

    Begin along the riverside cobbles of Carrera del Darro beneath the red walls of the Alhambra.

  2. 2

    El Bañuelo

    Step inside El Bañuelo, eleventh-century Zirid public baths lit by star-shaped ceiling vents.

  3. 3

    Mirador de San Nicolás

    Catch the sweeping sunset panorama of the Alhambra and Sierra Nevada from Mirador de San Nicolás.

  4. 4

    Plaza Larga and the Arco de las Pesas

    Climb into Plaza Larga beside the eleventh-century horseshoe gateway of the old Zirid wall.

  5. 5

    Sacromonte

    Finish on the prickly-pear slopes of Sacromonte, famed for its whitewashed cave dwellings and flamenco.
