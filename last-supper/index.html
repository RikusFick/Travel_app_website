Four focused stops make the most of the strictly timed fifteen-minute entry inside Santa Maria delle Grazie's refectory. Listen outdoors before entering so you know where to look, how Leonardo composed the moment, and how the fragile wall survived floods and WWII bombs.

Stops

  1. 1

    A duke's church

    Begin outside Santa Maria delle Grazie to see Bramante's brick tribune built for Duke Ludovico Sforza.

  2. 2

    Before you go in

    Orient yourself outside the refectory entrance before your timed slot, learning why Leonardo painted on dry plaster instead of true fresco.

  3. 3

    The moment

    Stand facing the end wall inside the refectory to trace the emotional shockwave across the twelve apostles grouped in threes.

  4. 4

    How it survived

    Compare Leonardo's fading tempera against Montorfano's crisp Crucifixion opposite, and hear how sandbags saved the wall in 1943.
