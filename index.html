What it does

A storyteller in your pocket

Keep your phone in your pocket and look around. Earwise plays the right story at the right stop. No tour group, no timetable, and no signal after you download.

Plays at each stop

GPS starts the story when you arrive. Your location stays on your phone.

Works offline

Download a guide once. The map and the stories work without roaming.

10 languages

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

How it works

Three steps, then just walk

  1. Pick a guide

    Choose a place in the app, or open Audio guides and pick a country.

  2. Download it

    Save the guide on your phone. The App Store and Google Play apps are coming soon.

  3. Explore

    Walk. Each story starts as you reach its stop. Your phone can stay in your pocket.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need mobile data?

Only to download a guide. After that it works offline, including the map.

Do you track where I go?

No. Location is used on your phone to play the right stop. We don’t collect it. See the privacy policy.

What does a guide cost?

Checkout is not open. Where a signed price is known, the page shows it (Moorish Castle is €1.99). Other cards say Soon. Older €2–5 labels are the earlier pay-once band, not a live price.

Which languages?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

When can I download it?
