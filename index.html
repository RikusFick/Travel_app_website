FAQ

Good to know

How does Earwise work?

You walk, and the story starts at each stop. Open a guide, press play, and in the app it begins as you arrive; here on the website you play each stop yourself.

Can I try a guide for free?

Yes. Free guides play in full, and every paid guide has a free first stop so you can hear it before you buy.

Can I listen before I travel?

Yes. Open a guide here in your browser whenever you like — at home, on the way, or at the place itself.

How do I get started?

Search for a place or browse the audio guides, then press play. You can listen in your browser today.

How long does a visit take?

Take as long as you like. Most visits take an hour or more. The stories play at each stop as you go, with time to look around in between, and you can pause or replay anytime.

Who writes the stories?

We research and fact-check every guide, and a second reviewer checks each one before it goes out.

Is my location private?

Yes. Your location stays on your phone, and we never collect where you walk. There's more detail in the privacy policy.
