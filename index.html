FAQ

Good to know

Can I listen offline on the website?

No. The website streams. Offline listening is in the app.

Do I need mobile data?

On the website, yes. In the app, only to download. Then it works offline.

Do you track where I go?

No. Location stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.

What does a guide cost?

Free, or the price on the page. Paid once.

Which languages?

Up to 10: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Hindi, and Chinese.
