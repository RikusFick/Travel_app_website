What it does

A storyteller in your pocket

Keep your phone in your pocket and look around. Listen online in your browser, or use the free Earwise app for offline guides and automatic playback at each stop.

Plays at each stop

GPS auto-play when you arrive is in the free Earwise app. Your location stays on your phone.

Online on web, offline in app

Guides bought on the website play online in the browser (with the read-along script). Offline listening and auto-play by location need the free Earwise app with the same account.

Up to 10 languages

How it works

Three steps, then just walk

  1. Pick a guide

    Browse 287 guides across 8 countries. Free guides and stop-1 samples play right in your browser.

  2. Listen online or in the app

    Guides bought on the website stream online with the read-along script. Sign in with the same account in the free Earwise app (coming soon) to download them offline.

  3. Explore

    Walk at your own pace. In the app, each story starts automatically as you reach its stop — no signal needed.

FAQ

Good to know

Can I listen offline on the website?

No — offline guides are app-only. Guides bought on the website play online in your browser (with the read-along script). Offline listening and auto-play by location need the free Earwise app with the same account.

Do I need mobile data?

On the website, yes, because audio streams in your browser. In the Earwise app, you only need data to download a guide — after that, the stories and map work offline.

Do you track where I go?

No. In the app, location stays on your phone to trigger stops and show you on the map. We don’t collect it. See the privacy policy.

What does a guide cost?

Checkout is not open yet. Guide pages show the guide price (Free, or €1.99–€3.99, paid once) for open countries. Coming-soon countries do not have prices published yet.

Which languages?

Most guides are recorded in all ten: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Hindi and Chinese. A few United Arab Emirates guides have fewer, or none yet. There is no Korean audio.

When can I download the app?

Not yet. The apps are not in the App Store or Google Play yet — the buttons above email hello@getearwise.com instead of opening a store.
