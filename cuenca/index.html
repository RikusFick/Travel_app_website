Explore a cliff-top medieval town balanced on a limestone spur between the Júcar and Huécar river gorges. Five stops follow the cobbled spine from Plaza Mayor and Spain's earliest Gothic cathedral out onto the iron footbridge facing the Hanging Houses.

Stops

  1. 1

    Plaza Mayor of Cuenca

    Start in the sloping pastel square at the summit of Cuenca's narrow rocky ridge.

  2. 2

    Cuenca Cathedral

    Step into the Anglo-Norman Gothic nave of Cuenca Cathedral, begun in the late twelfth century.

  3. 3

    The Hanging Houses

    View the surviving Casas Colgadas whose wooden balconies overhang the sheer drop of the Huécar gorge.

  4. 4

    San Pablo Bridge

    Cross the high iron-and-plank footbridge linking the old town cliff to the San Pablo Parador.

  5. 5

    Torre de Mangana

    Finish at the square clock tower standing on the site of Cuenca's Moorish alcázar.
