Step inside the 1904 townhouse transformation that turned a plain Passeig de Gràcia apartment building into shimmering glass, bone-like stone, and ceramic scales. Seven stops take you from street level up to the tiled dragon spine on the roof.

Stops

  1. 1

    The façade of Casa Batlló

    Read the carnival-mask balconies and shimmering glass discs across the street façade.

  2. 2

    The Block of Discord

    Compare three rival Modernista façades standing side by side on Barcelona's Block of Discord.

  3. 3

    The entrance hall and staircase

    Enter the submarine-like stairwell where curved wood handrails climb past organic shapes.

  4. 4

    The noble floor

    Explore the Batlló family's main living room with its huge oak windows and swirling plaster ceiling.

  5. 5

    The light well

    Look up the central tiled shaft whose blues deepen toward the skylight to balance natural daylight.

  6. 6

    The loft of arches

    Walk beneath white catenary arches built to keep the laundry loft cool and airy.

  7. 7

    The dragon roof

    Climb onto the terrace crowned by arched ceramic tiles evoking the back of a sleeping dragon.
