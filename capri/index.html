Nine offline stops cover Capri from harbour arrival to hilltop Roman ruins and coastal sea caves. Follow funiculars, cliffside zigzag paths, Emperor Tiberius's cliff palace, Anacapri's Villa San Michele, and the glowing Blue Grotto.

Stops

  1. 1

    An island of goats, or boars

    Start at Marina Grande harbour to hear how ancient Greeks and Romans turned this limestone rock into an imperial playground.

  2. 2

    The Piazzetta

    Ride up to the Piazzetta, Capri's tiny marble-paved living room overlooking the Bay of Naples.

  3. 3

    The monks behind the wall

    Pass the fourteenth-century Certosa di San Giacomo, a peaceful former Carthusian monastery tucked behind stone walls.

  4. 4

    A millionaire's garden

    Stroll the terraced Gardens of Augustus, laid out in the early twentieth century by German industrialist Friedrich Alfred Krupp.

  5. 5

    Via Krupp

    Peer down over Via Krupp, the dramatic hairpinned footpath blasted straight down the cliff face to Marina Piccola.

  6. 6

    The Faraglioni

    Take in the classic view of the three towering limestone Faraglioni sea stacks from the Belvedere di Tragara.

  7. 7

    Villa Jovis

    Climb Monte Tiberio to Villa Jovis, the largest of Emperor Tiberius's twelve island villas perched atop sheer sea cliffs.

  8. 8

    Anacapri and Villa San Michele

    Head up to Anacapri's Villa San Michele, filled with antiquities collected by Swedish physician Axel Munthe.

  9. 9

    The Blue Grotto

    Finish at the sea-level Blue Grotto, where sunlight entering through a submerged cavern lights the water vivid electric blue.
