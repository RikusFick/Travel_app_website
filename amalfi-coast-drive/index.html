    Read along

    You're now on the Amalfi Coast, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997, and one of the most beautiful coastlines on Earth.

    The road you're on is the only land route along this coast: about forty kilometres of curves, carved into the cliffs from Positano to Vietri sul Mare.

    Below is Positano, its houses stacked down the cliff to the beach.

    Inside the church of Santa Maria Assunta, down by the beach, is a Byzantine icon of a black Madonna. Local tradition holds that pirates were carrying it across the sea when a storm blew up, and the sailors heard a voice say "Posa, posa!": "Put it down! Put it down!". They carried the icon ashore, the storm died away, and the town, the tradition says, took its name from those words.

    The dome of Santa Maria Assunta is covered in majolica, glazed ceramic tiles, a decoration you'll see on church domes all along this coast.
