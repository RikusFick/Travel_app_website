A 5-stop offline audio guide to Koutoubia & Jemaa el-Fna in Marrakech, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers the twelfth-century Almohad minaret and the open-air square of storytellers and food stalls. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.

Stops

  1. 1

    The Koutoubia minaret

    On the paved esplanade on the north-east side of the mosque, east of the minaret, with the full height of the tower in view. This is stop 1 on the route.

  2. 2

    The mosque and its prayer hall

    Beside the mosque walls, on the garden side. This is stop 2 on the route.

  3. 3

    The ruins of the first mosque

    At the low excavated walls on the northern side of the mosque. This is stop 3 on the route.

  4. 4

    Jemaa el-Fna by day

    On the open square, with the souk entrances to the north. This is stop 4 on the route.
