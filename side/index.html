A 7-stop offline audio guide to Side in Side, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers the peninsula ruins, grand theatre, and seaside Temple of Apollo. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.

Stops

  1. 1

    The pomegranate city

    Beside the large square agora (marketplace), next to the theatre and opposite the museum. This is stop 1 on the route.

  2. 2

    The theatre

    Outside the high outer walls of the theatre, at the narrowest point of the peninsula. This is stop 2 on the route.

  3. 3

    The monumental gate and the Vespasian monument

    On the main road into old Side, beside the theatre, at the tall Roman archway, with the small columned fountain monument set against the wall next to it. This is stop 3 on the route.

  4. 4

    A museum in a Roman bath

    In front of the Side Museum, the restored Roman bath building opposite the agora. This is stop 4 on the route.

  5. 5

    A village on the ruins

    This stop is given to A village on the ruins.

  6. 6

    The temples by the sea

    Stand here for the story of The temples by the sea.

  7. 7

    The Nymphaeum (optional)

    Outside the old city walls, beside the high ruined fountain front opposite the main city gate, near the road into Side. This is stop 7 on the route.
