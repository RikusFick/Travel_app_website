    The First Courtyard

    In the large, park-like First Courtyard, with the old Byzantine church of Hagia Irene, a red-brick building with a dome, on your left. This is stop 2 on the route.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  3. 3

    Hagia Irene

    In front of the red-brick Byzantine church with a large dome on the left-hand side of the First Courtyard. This is stop 3 on the route.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  4. 4

    The Gate of Salutation

    In front of the gateway with two tall, pointed octagonal towers: the Bâbüsselâm, or Gate of Salutation, also called the Middle Gate. This is stop 4 on the route.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  5. 5

    The Second Courtyard

    In the middle of the Second Courtyard, a wide, green square with paths and trees, surrounded by colonnades. The kitchens with their row of chimneys are on your right, the Imperial Council and tall Tower of Justice on your left, and the Gate of Felicity straight ahead. This is stop 5 on the route.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  6. 6

    The Palace Kitchens

    Under the colonnade on the right-hand (east) side of the Second Courtyard, in front of the long row of kitchen buildings with their tall chimneys. This is stop 6 on the route.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  7. 7

    The Imperial Council

    This modest-looking building was the government of an empire.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  8. 8

    The Outer Treasury and the arms collection

    This long building with its eight domes is the Outer Treasury, and it was the state's strongroom.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  9. 9

    The Gate of Felicity

    In front of the ornate gate at the end of the Second Courtyard, with a deep, painted canopy roof and a golden ball hanging in the middle: the Bâbüssaâde, or Gate of Felicity. This is stop 9 on the route.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  10. 10

    The Audience Chamber

    Just inside the Third Courtyard, in front of the square pavilion with a wide overhanging roof on columns: the Arz Odası, or Audience Chamber.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  11. 11

    The Library of Ahmed III

    In the middle of the Third Courtyard, behind the Audience Chamber, in front of the small, elegant marble building with a fountain under its porch: the Library of Ahmed III. This is stop 11 on the route.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  12. 12

    The Imperial Treasury

    At the Pavilion of the Conqueror, the Fatih Köşkü, in the right-hand corner of the Third Courtyard, which houses the Imperial Treasury.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  13. 13

    The Chamber of the Sacred Relics

    Take in the surroundings here during stop 13.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  14. 14

    The Revan Kiosk and İftariye

    On the marble terrace of the Fourth Courtyard, beside the pool, between the Revan Kiosk and the small gilded İftariye canopy overlooking the Golden Horn. This is stop 14 on the route.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  15. 15

    The Baghdad Kiosk

    The domed pavilion at the end of the terrace is the Baghdad Kiosk.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  16. 16

    The Harem (optional)

    At the Harem entrance next to the Tower of Justice, in the Second Courtyard, or inside the first rooms of the Harem.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  17. 17

    The Imperial Hall (Harem)

    Inside the Harem, in the great domed hall with the sultan's throne and a gallery above: the Hünkâr Sofası, or Imperial Hall. (Indoor.). This is stop 17 on the route.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  18. 18

    The Privy Chamber of Murad III (Harem)

    This tiled, domed room is the Privy Chamber of Murad the Third, the oldest and finest room in the Harem.

    Script and audio included when you buy
  19. 19

    The Twin Kiosk (Harem)

    Two seventeenth-century rooms on a high platform, built so those inside could look out and no one outside could look in.

    Script and audio included when you buy

    Topkapı's legacy

    Anywhere on your way back towards the exit, or on a bench in the Second Courtyard.
