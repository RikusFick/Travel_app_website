Explore the largest Gothic cathedral on earth, raised atop Seville's great twelfth-century Almohad mosque while keeping its minaret and orange courtyard intact. Ten stops cover the Giralda ramps, Columbus's tomb, the towering gilded retablo, and the Court of the Oranges.

Stops

  1. 1

    The Giralda

    Look up at the Giralda, an Almohad minaret crowned with a Renaissance belfry and rotating weathervane.

  2. 2

    The Tomb of Columbus

    Stand beside the monumental bronze-and-marble tomb carried by four heraldic kingdoms for Christopher Columbus.

  3. 3

    The Main Sacristy and Arfe's Monstrance

    See Juan de Arfe's towering silver Corpus Christi monstrance inside the vaulted Main Sacristy.

  4. 4

    The Chapter House

    Step into the oval Chapter House, designed with perspective-tricking marble floors and Murillo roundels.

  5. 5

    The Royal Chapel

    Visit the domed Royal Chapel holding the remains of Ferdinand III and Alfonso X the Wise.

  6. 6

    The Nave, Choir and Organ

    Take in the soaring five-aisled Gothic nave, carved walnut choir, and monumental pipe organs.

  7. 7

    The High Altarpiece

    Study the world's largest altarpiece, a 20-metre wall of gilded cedar scenes from the life of Christ.

  8. 8

    Murillo's Vision of Saint Anthony

    Pause before Murillo's dramatic canvas of Saint Anthony of Padua bathed in heavenly light.

  9. 9

    The Court of the Oranges

    Walk out into the Almohad Courtyard of the Oranges, shaded by bitter citrus trees and irrigation channels.

  10. 10

    The Door of Forgiveness

    Exit beneath the horseshoe Door of Forgiveness, guarded by Renaissance terracotta reliefs.
