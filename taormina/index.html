Eight stops lead from Taormina's cliffside Ancient Theatre facing Mount Etna along Corso Umberto to Isola Bella below. Trace Greek drama, Roman brick renovations, medieval palazzi, and Victorian gardens. Listen here, or get the Earwise app to play as you walk.
