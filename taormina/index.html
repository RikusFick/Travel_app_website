Eight offline stops lead from Taormina's cliffside Ancient Theatre facing Mount Etna along Corso Umberto to Isola Bella below. Trace Greek drama, Roman brick renovations, medieval palazzi, and Victorian gardens.

Stops

  1. 1

    A theatre with a view

    Begin at the top tier of the Ancient Theatre to take in the sweeping backdrop of the Ionian coast and Mount Etna.

  2. 2

    From drama to gladiators

    Descend toward the brick stage wall to see how Roman builders converted a Greek theatre into an arena for spectacles.

  3. 3

    The gate and the palace

    Enter the old town at Porta Messina beside medieval Palazzo Corvaja, meeting place of Sicily's fifteenth-century parliament.

  4. 4

    The mystery wall

    Detour just below Corso Umberto to the Naumachia, a monumental 122-metre Roman brick retaining wall with eighteen niches.

  5. 5

    The balcony square

    Pause on the checkerboard terrace of Piazza IX Aprile for wide balcony views over Naxos Bay and the hilltop castle.

  6. 6

    The cathedral and the fountain

    Walk to Piazza Duomo by Taormina's crenellated stone cathedral and seventeenth-century centaur fountain.

  7. 7

    Lady Florence's garden

    Wander through the Villa Comunale public gardens planted by Scottish-born naturalist Lady Florence Trevelyan.

  8. 8

    Isola Bella

    Finish overlooking Isola Bella, the tiny nature-reserve islet linked to the pebble cove by a shifting sandbar.
