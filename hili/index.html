A 7-stop offline audio guide to Hili in Al Ain, recorded in 10 languages and designed to play as you arrive. It covers the 4,000-year-old Grand Tomb reliefs and Bronze Age oasis settlements of Hili Park. Download once; you will not need a signal on the ground.
